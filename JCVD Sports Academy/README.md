# JCVD Sports Academy — Dubai

A dual-concept sports venture co-founded by Jean-Claude Van Damme, based in Dubai, UAE (Est. 2026):

1. **The Academy** — full-time residential sports academy for athletes aged 4–18, combining elite coaching, KHDA-accredited academics, and a pathway to professional sport.
2. **The Sports Club** — a premium public sports club open to all ages, with flexible day/monthly/annual/family memberships.

Six sports: Football, Basketball, Martial Arts (JCVD's signature programme), Swimming, Tennis, Athletics.

## Key numbers (financial model)

- Year 1 Revenue: AED 16.5M → Year 5: AED 84M
- EBITDA breakeven: Year 2
- Year 3 EBITDA: ~AED 20–29M
- Total CAPEX: AED ~111M (midpoint), funded via AED 30M partner equity, AED 15M JCVD equity (brand + cash), AED 35M bank debt, AED 10M government/free-zone incentives, AED 10M seed investor round.
- Academy fees range AED 85,000–185,000/year (KG → Secondary, residential included).
- Club membership: AED 150 day pass / AED 899 monthly / AED 7,500 annual / AED 12,000 family.
